REVIVAL CINEMA AI — Prototype 0.1

WHAT THIS IS
A mobile-friendly browser prototype for the first phase of the Revival Cinema AI project.
It turns a pasted script into editable scene cards, suggests initial visual prompts, tracks scene status,
and exports JSON/CSV shot lists. It runs locally in your browser; no account or server is needed to test it.

HOW TO USE
1. Unzip the folder.
2. Open index.html in a modern browser (Chrome on Android or a desktop browser).
3. Paste your script and tap "Create scene plan".
4. Edit narration and visual prompts, set scene statuses, and export JSON or CSV.
5. "Save in this browser" saves to that browser/device only. Export copies for backup.

IMPORTANT LIMITS
- This is a functional prototype, not the complete AI video studio.
- It does not yet generate voice, subtitles, AI video clips, or a finished MP4.
- AI video generation will need a connected provider or worker/computer with suitable compute.
- Website hosting alone does not provide unlimited free AI video generation.
- Verify historical claims and label illustrative AI reenactments clearly before publishing.
